# EXP-012: A-vs-A — does the audit-plugin stand measure signal or noise?

- **Module:** audit-plugin
- **Status:** landed (stand calibration) — caught a false promotion verdict;
  noise floor measured at 2.75/task
- **Failure pattern:** n/a — the mandatory first run on a new benchmark, same
  duty as EXP-011 for the sibling module.
- **Change (ONE thing):** none. Both arms are `variants/champion`; arm B ran
  `--no-cache` so it could not replay arm A.
- **Prediction:** recall tie, with a wider noise band than EXP-011.
- **Success gate:** recall delta indistinguishable from zero.

## Setup

- Executor: `cursor-cli` / composer-2.5. The same stated limitation as EXP-011 —
  production spawns Claude subagents, so transfer is assumed here.
- Judge: the paid CLI fallback (gpt-5.2), uncalibrated against the
  `audit-findings` shape.
- Benchmark: 4 dev tasks (3 positive, 10 must-find items + 1 negative),
  rubrics v1, on the `screen_facets` subset (staleness, logic, safety, wiring).

## Runs

| stage | run dir | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A-vs-A (dev, 1 trial each) | `runs/scratch/ap-a` vs `runs/scratch/ap-b` | recall tie; noise reported a WIN between identical arms |
| re-judge, panel of three | same findings, rescored | noise -3.75 -> -2.75/task |

**recall_must:** mean delta 0, 0 wins / 0 losses / 3 ties, CI95 [0, 0], MDE 0.
Both arms scored 0.889 — plugin-2 misses the same must-find in each, so unlike
the sibling module this benchmark still has recall headroom.

**noise:** single judge call — mean delta -3.75/task, CI95 [-6.0, -1.48].
Panel of three — mean delta **-2.75/task**, CI95 [-4.0, -0.99]. Recorded as
this module's noise-of-noise band.

Per task (A / B), single call: plugin-1 3/6 · plugin-2 2/8 · plugin-3 0/0 ·
plugin-neg-1 2/8.

`compare.sh` printed, for two byte-identical variants:

> "candidate better on noise, recall held"

## Decision

**This is the failure an A-vs-A exists to catch, caught.** Had that verdict
appeared under a real variant, the promotion gate would have fired on a change
that did nothing, and the ledger would have recorded a win.

The direction points opposite to EXP-011's, which is itself the evidence: the
sign of the "effect" is not a property of the stand. Residue sets of similar
size (58 in arm A, 68 in arm B) were bucketed 5 noise / 53 plausible-real in one
arm and 21 / 47 in the other.

Two consequences, and one is about the harness rather than this module:

1. **Part of that split was the judge, and part was not.** EXP-011 separated
   them by re-judging one arm's findings six times: match verdicts identical
   every time, residue bucketing swinging 1.5/task. A panel of three cuts the
   judge's share and moves this module's delta from -3.75 to -2.75; the rest is
   the executor flagging genuinely different beyond-rubric material at one trial
   per arm.

2. **`compare.sh` could promote on noise alone, and now cannot.** Its
   "candidate better on noise, recall held" branch fired on a CI below zero
   with recall merely not-worse. A-vs-A runs do not generally bracket zero on
   this axis — review's own is [0.29, 1.13] — so the CI was never the right
   test. EXP-001 had already settled on a magnitude band (~1.1/task) and left
   it in prose; it is now `noise_floor` in each module's target.json, and a
   delta inside the band reads as "not an effect" rather than as a win.

   Re-run against the same two identical arms, the verdict now reads: *"noise
   delta 2.75/task is inside the module's measured noise-of-noise band
   (2.75) — not an effect."*
