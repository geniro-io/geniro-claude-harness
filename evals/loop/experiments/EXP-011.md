# EXP-011: A-vs-A — does the audit-instructions stand measure signal or noise?

- **Module:** audit-instructions
- **Status:** landed (stand calibration) — recall usable, noise floor measured at 2.6/task
- **Failure pattern:** n/a — the mandatory first run on a new benchmark
  (DESIGN.md §Statistics). Champion against champion must come back a tie; the
  CI width is the noise floor every later verdict on this module is read
  against.
- **Change (ONE thing):** none. Both arms are `variants/champion`, same prompts.
  Arm B ran `--no-cache`, or identical prompts replay arm A's responses and the
  tie is an artifact of the cache rather than a measurement.
- **Prediction:** recall tie; noise spread of roughly 1 finding per task.
- **Success gate:** recall delta indistinguishable from zero.

## Setup

- Executor: `cursor-cli` / composer-2.5, the designed screening tier.
  **Stated limitation:** production `/geniro:audit-instructions` spawns
  general-purpose Claude subagents at the orchestrator's tier, so this stand
  measures the criteria text's effect on a capable agent of a different family.
  Cross-executor transfer is assumed here, not measured.
- Judge: the paid CLI fallback (gpt-5.2), a different family from the executor,
  never calibrated against the `audit-findings` output shape. Scored twice: once
  on a single call, once on a panel of three (see Decision).
- Benchmark: 5 dev tasks (4 positive, 17 must-find items + 1 negative),
  rubrics v1-v3, all 5 facets.

## Runs

| stage | run dir | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A-vs-A (dev, 1 trial each) | `runs/scratch/ai-a` vs `runs/scratch/ai-b` | recall tie; noise floor 2.6/task |
| re-judge, panel of three | same findings, rescored | noise 3.6 -> 2.6/task |

**recall_must:** mean delta 0, 0 wins / 0 losses / 4 ties, CI95 [0, 0], MDE 0.
Both arms scored **1.0 on every positive task**.

**noise:** single judge call — mean delta 3.6/task, CI95 [1.2, 6.2], MDE 2.5.
Panel of three — mean delta **2.6/task**, CI95 [0.6, 4.2], MDE 1.8. Neither
brackets zero, which is normal for this axis (review's own floor is
[0.29, 1.13]); 2.6/task is therefore recorded as this module's noise-of-noise
band in `target.json`, and a candidate has to beat it to be read as an effect.

## Decision

**Recall compares cleanly; noise compares only against a floor.** Two findings,
and the second is the one that matters.

1. **Recall is saturated.** Every must-find item is caught in both arms, so the
   MDE of 0 is coverage of a ceiling, not sensitivity — there is nowhere for a
   recall improvement to land. The fixtures are too easy for this executor. The
   fix is the one main applied to the partition module: plant subtler defects
   until the champion misses some.

2. **The noise axis carries two variance sources, and only one was fixable
   here.** Re-judging one arm's findings six times, same prompt and same
   findings, separated them: match verdicts came back identical every time,
   while residue bucketing swung 1.5/task between two calls. A panel of three
   cuts that to 0.5/task, and it took the between-arm delta from 3.6 to 2.6.

   The 2.6 that remains is the executor: two identical arms genuinely flag
   different beyond-rubric material, and one trial per arm does not average it.
   DESIGN.md's own screening minimum is 2 trials, and this is the reason.

**The stand can compare recall and cannot presently promote anything**, because
the benchmark has no recall headroom and a noise effect must now clear
2.6/task. Both are fixable and neither is silent: de-saturate the fixtures, and
re-measure the floor at 2-3 trials.
