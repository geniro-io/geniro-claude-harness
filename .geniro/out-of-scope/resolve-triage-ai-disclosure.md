# AI-disclosure line on /geniro:resolve replies

Open every PR reply and thread resolution that `/geniro:resolve` posts with a line saying an AI wrote it.

## Why this is out of scope

Disclosure is the project's call. A project that requires it carries the rule in its own instructions (`.geniro/instructions/`, `CLAUDE.md`) and the skill applies it; a plugin-wide line would override projects that do not want one.
