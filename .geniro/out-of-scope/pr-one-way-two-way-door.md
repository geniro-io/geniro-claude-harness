# One-way / two-way door framing in PR bodies

Label each change in a PR body a one-way or two-way door, with how to roll it back.

## Why this is out of scope

The Merge danger item in `skills/_shared/pr-body.md` names only what cannot be undone and forbids "safe", "low-risk" and "easy to roll back" framing: reassurance lowers a reviewer's defect detection (`skills/_shared/review-brief.md` §Content contract). A two-way-door label is rollback framing.
