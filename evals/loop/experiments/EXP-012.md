# EXP-012: A-vs-A — does the audit-plugin stand measure signal or noise?

- **Module:** audit-plugin
- **Status:** landed (stand calibration, no skill change) — **the noise axis
  failed, and failed loudly enough to emit a false promotion verdict**
- **Failure pattern:** n/a — the mandatory first run on a new benchmark, same
  duty as EXP-011 for the sibling module.
- **Change (ONE thing):** none. Both arms are `variants/champion`; arm B ran
  `--no-cache` so it could not replay arm A.
- **Prediction:** recall tie, with a wider noise band than EXP-011.
- **Success gate:** recall delta indistinguishable from zero.

## Setup

- Executor: `cursor-cli` / composer-2.5. The same stated limitation as EXP-011 —
  production spawns Claude subagents, so transfer is assumed here.
- Judge: the paid CLI fallback (gpt-5.2), uncalibrated against the
  `audit-findings` shape.
- Benchmark: 4 dev tasks (3 positive, 10 must-find items + 1 negative),
  rubrics v1, on the `screen_facets` subset (staleness, logic, safety, wiring).

## Decision

**This is the failure an A-vs-A exists to catch, caught.** Had that verdict
appeared under a real variant, the promotion gate would have fired on a change
that did nothing, and the ledger would have recorded a win.

The mechanism is the same as EXP-011's and points the opposite way, which is
itself the evidence: the direction of the "effect" is not a property of the
stand, it is a coin flip. Residue sets of similar size (58 in arm A, 68 in arm
B) were bucketed 5 noise / 53 plausible-real in one arm and 21 / 47 in the
other. What moves is the judge's 3-way call on findings with no ground truth to
check against, not anything the executor did.

Two consequences, and one is about the harness rather than this module:

1. **No audit-module verdict may cite noise until the judge is calibrated for
   this output shape.** `judge/calibration/kappa.py` is the instrument.

2. **`compare.sh`'s verdict string can promote on noise alone.** Its
   "candidate better on noise, recall held" branch fires on a noise CI below
   zero with recall merely not-worse. That is sound only where the noise metric
   has been shown to reproduce. The A-vs-A is what shows it, so the verdict
   should refuse to read noise on a module whose recorded noise floor does not
   bracket zero — a stand-condition gate, not a statistics change.
